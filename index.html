K-IsoTrace Privacy Policy

Effective : August 4, 2026 · Last updated : October 2, 2026

In one line
K-IsoTrace has no user accounts, and the drawings you create never leave your device. The developer cannot see them. The app shows no ads. Outbound traffic is used only for checking the free trial, usage statistics, receiving app settings, and subscription payments (Google Play).

2. Information the app processes

K-IsoTrace has no sign-up or login. It does not collect your name, phone number, email address, postal address, photos, or the content of your drawings. The table below is everything the app sends off the device.

WhatWhyWhere · how long
A hash of the device identifier
(SHA-256 of the device ID; the original value is never sent)
+ first-use date · new/existing user flag		 So the free trial (30 days) and the existing-user benefit apply once per device Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) · kept to prevent repeated trials; you may request deletion by email
Usage statistics
(app installation ID, screen/feature usage counts, device model, OS, language, country-level location)		 To understand which features are used and improve the app Google Analytics for Firebase · retained under Google's policies; the developer sees aggregated data only
Settings request data
(app installation ID, country, language, OS version)		 To receive settings such as the trial length without an app update Google Firebase (Remote Config)
Subscription payments Processing the PRO subscription (monthly) Handled directly by Google Play. Card numbers and other payment details are never passed to the developer; the app only checks whether a subscription is active

All of the above is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and is never sold or shared with third parties (other than processing by Google to provide these services). See Privacy and Security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy.
